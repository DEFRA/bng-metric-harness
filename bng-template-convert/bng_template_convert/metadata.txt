about=Moves a habitat map between the two BNG QGIS templates without anyone needing a command line.
    Convert to legacy splits a BNG Service GeoPackage into the baseline and post-intervention pair the older Biodiversity Metric service expects.
    Convert from legacy joins a legacy baseline and post-intervention pair back into a single BNG Service GeoPackage, rebuilding as much baseline-to-post-intervention lineage as the legacy files allow and reporting exactly what had to be inferred.
    Both tools appear in the Processing Toolbox and under Plugins > BNG Template Convert. No dependencies beyond QGIS itself.
